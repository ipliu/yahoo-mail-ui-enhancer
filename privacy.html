01

At a glance

This extension operates offline. It changes the appearance of eligible Yahoo Mail pages and remembers the options you select.

No telemetry. No analytics. No remote configuration. No network requests.

02

What stays on your device

The extension uses your browser’s local extension storage only to retain its settings. These values do not leave your browser through this extension.

Sidebar preference
Whether the Yahoo Mail sidebar is concealed.
Unread emphasis
Whether unread messages receive stronger emphasis in dark mode.
03

What we do not collect

The extension does not collect or transmit personal information, including:

  • Email content, subjects, attachments, or recipient information
  • Account identifiers, passwords, or authentication information
  • Browsing activity, usage data, or device information
  • Diagnostics, crash reports, analytics, or advertising identifiers
04

Scope

This policy applies to Yahoo Mail UI Enhancer. The extension is independent and unofficial; it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Yahoo. Yahoo’s own services remain governed by Yahoo’s applicable policies.
